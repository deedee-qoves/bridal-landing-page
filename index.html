Frequently asked questions

If you have any further questions, contact us at hello@qoves.com.

Will I really see results?+

Yes, when you follow your plan. Every step is chosen for your face and timed so the changes build on each other, so by the day they add up to something you can see. How much you see depends on where you start and how closely you follow it.

Are the visualizations realistic?+

Yes. We only show changes your plan can achieve without surgery, using your own photos. Think of them as a preview of you at your best, not a filter. Results vary from person to person.

Do I need surgery?+

No. Every recommendation in your plan is non-surgical, from skincare and treatments to hair and makeup direction. Anything involving clinical services, procedures or medication should be discussed with a licensed provider.

Does it work for all ages and ethnicities?+

Yes. Your analysis takes into account your heritage, your age and how your features change over time, so your plan suits your face, not a one-size-fits-all standard.

I have product preferences. Can my plan work around them?+

Yes. Tell us what matters to you, like vegan or cruelty-free, and your plan will take it into account. You can message our care team any time if something does not suit you.

My wedding is soon. Is that okay?+

Yes. Your plan is built around your date. With less time, it focuses on the steps that make the biggest difference before the day. The earlier you start, the more you can do, so it is worth starting now.

Time to look and feel your best.

The morning of the wedding should feel exciting, not stressful. With your plan behind you, you can relax into the build up, laugh with the people around you and stop thinking about your face altogether. The photos will take care of themselves.

Will analyzing my face make me insecure?

You learn what's truly unique about your face.

Many features you might worry about are actually positive traits. Our analysis often helps people appreciate aspects of their face they used to dislike.

You get clarity about what can actually be improved.

Slight facial asymmetry is perfectly normal. Everyone has it and it should not be corrected. But if uneven skin tone is making you self conscious, that is something we can help improve.

You feel better about how you look.

Many members tell us they come away more confident. Once you understand your face and know what suits it, you spend less time doubting it and more time enjoying it.
